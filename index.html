💢 PANEL 05 — THE WORLD DIDN'T GO EASY
📖 NARRATOR Here's what the village records don't show: the missions that failed. The nights that felt too long. The moments where the path forward looked like a wall.

Draksmer knows those walls. He's headbutted every single one of them. Sometimes the wall won round one. But round two? Round two always belonged to him.

⚔️ TRIAL 1 — "The Forest of Death" Moment

Every shinobi has a Forest of Death — that stretch where nothing goes right, where the strongest people look at you like you don't belong. Draksmer walked through his. Didn't run. Didn't quit. Came out the other side, scratched up and absolutely still standing.

💔 TRIAL 2 — "The Sasuke Moment"

There's always a Sasuke — someone who leaves, a door that closes, a thing you wanted that didn't work out. The kind of hurt that makes you question everything. Draksmer felt that. Deeply. But unlike most, he didn't let it hollow him out. He let it shape him instead.

🌑 TRIAL 3 — "The Pain Arc"

Nagato once destroyed the whole village to prove a point. Life hits like that sometimes — overwhelming, unfair, total. But remember: after the Pain Arc? Naruto rebuilt every single house. You have that same rebuild energy, Draksmer. Always have.
